Fit-session guidance modal

Three high-fidelity directions for preventing customers from accidentally starting a duplicate fit session. Use the tabs to compare approaches; choose “Add video” inside a concept to preview the confirmation step.

Concept A — Guided choices

Balanced, familiar and closest to the task brief.

Recommended baseline
Why explore it: Both paths remain equally understandable, while a subtle “Recommended” label guides most returning customers toward the correct action. Lowest implementation and comprehension risk.

Concept B — Recommended action first

Uses the latest completed fit as the primary object and minimizes decision effort.

Strongest behavioral nudge
Why explore it: Makes the preferred path feel concrete by showing the bike and session immediately. Best when the latest completed fit is usually the correct target.

Concept C — Side-by-side decision

Explicit comparison makes the rule easy to learn and remember.

Most educational
Why explore it: The parallel layout clearly teaches “same bike and goal” versus “different bike or goal.” It is the most visually explanatory, although slightly denser on desktop.
